# Quizzes for Instructors

If you are teaching with this book, you can import its quizzes into your learning
management system: a formative quiz for each of Chapters 1 to 18, and a summative quiz
for the whole book. Every question is multiple choice, and every answer comes with
feedback that explains why it is right or wrong.

The files are in the [`quizzes` folder](https://github.com/AllenDowney/ThinkPython/tree/v3/quizzes)
of the book's repository, which also has instructions for importing them.

* **Canvas:** [QTI 1.2 zips](https://github.com/AllenDowney/ThinkPython/tree/v3/quizzes/canvas), one per quiz.
* **Moodle:** [Moodle XML](https://github.com/AllenDowney/ThinkPython/tree/v3/quizzes/moodle), one per quiz or all in one file.
* **Brightspace (D2L):** a [Question Library CSV](https://github.com/AllenDowney/ThinkPython/tree/v3/quizzes/brightspace), or the Canvas QTI zips.

The Canvas files have been tested. The Moodle and Brightspace files follow those
systems' documented formats but have not yet been tried on a real installation; if you
use them, please [let me know how it goes](https://github.com/AllenDowney/ThinkPython/issues).
